Gabrial Zencha Ashungafac

Research Associate, Carnegie Mellon University Africa  ·  Research Scientist (part-time), Intron Health

I am a machine learning researcher at the Carnegie Mellon University Africa (VLI Lab) in Kigali, Rwanda. My work focuses on learning from noisy, heterogeneous, high-dimensional signals: modeling, transforming, and extracting structure from speech, time-series sensor data, and visual data.

Much of my recent work is on speech recognition for African languages and accents: building benchmarks such as AfriSpeech-MultiBench and AfriSwitch, and developing production ASR systems at Intron Health covering 15 African languages. I also work on the reliability of large language models (selection bias, domain adaptation) and on robust computer vision (face anti-spoofing, low-resource OCR).

My background is in signal processing and stochastic modeling. I hold an MSc in Engineering Artificial Intelligence from Carnegie Mellon University and a BSc in Computer Engineering (Higher Honors) from TED University, Ankara. My work has appeared at Interspeech, IJCNLP–AACL, ICCV Workshops, ICLR Workshops, and IEEE Globecom.
