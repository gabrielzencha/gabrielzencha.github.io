summary

Machine learning researcher with a strong foundation in signal processing, stochastic modeling, and learning under noisy and heterogeneous data. My work focuses on modeling, transforming, and extracting structure from high-dimensional signals, including time-series sensor data, speech, and visual data, with applications in environmental sensing, speech technologies, and computer vision. Published at Interspeech, IJCNLP–AACL, ICCV Workshops, ICLR Workshops, and IEEE Globecom.
