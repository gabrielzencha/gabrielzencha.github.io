teaching & service

Teaching, talks, mentoring, and service to the research community.

Teaching is one of the parts of academic life I enjoy most. I have volunteered as a teaching assistant every semester since 2024, I lecture whenever I get the chance, and I like helping the people around me grow, from mentoring student projects to working through a proof with a classmate. In the long run I want to be a professor, so that this work reaches further than my own research.
